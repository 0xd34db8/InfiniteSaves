Where to Find Infinite Lives Save.bytes Files

Locate your save file on your device and drag it into Infinite Saves:

  • Steam / Windows PC: SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/Infinite Lives/Save.bytes or %USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/MDickie/Infinite Lives/
  • Android: Android/data/com.MDickie.InfiniteLives/files/Save.bytes
  • Third-Party Decompiled Saves: Supports .json and .txt files decompiled via saveeditonline.com.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I edit my Infinite Lives Save.bytes file?
Open Infinite Saves in any browser, drag and drop your Save.bytes file, customize your character's stats and superpowers, then click "Export Save.bytes" to download your patched save ready to play.
Is Infinite Saves safe and private?
Yes. Everything is processed entirely in your web browser's memory using JavaScript ArrayBuffers. No files are uploaded to any server or third party.
